 WHY (29) FAILS HERE.  With d_i = d_j = d and dtheta = d the four
 arguments are a1 = -d, a2 = +d, a3 = 0, a4 = 2d.  The third series is
 SUM cos(0)/n = SUM 1/n, the harmonic series: (29) evaluates
 ln|2 sin(a3/2)| = ln 0 = -Inf and the entry has no finite limit.
 (29) at a3 = 0 gives : -Inf
 This is not a degenerate corner case: on a contiguous tiling it is the
 generic situation of the two largest off-diagonal entries of EVERY row.
 The correct statement at the first neighbour is (30b), which carries the
