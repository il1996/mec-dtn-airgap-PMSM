  RECONCILIATION. The two figures are not in conflict; they count different
  work. The 0.06 ms marginal cost is ONE back-substitution against an already
  factorised operator, and nothing else. The 4.16 ms per position is a whole
  cogging_mec call divided by its positions, and that call also assembles the
  operator once, factorises once, and then, at EVERY position, rebuilds the
  source, reconstructs Br and Bt over the bore and forms the Maxwell stress.
  The measured split above says how much of the gap each part accounts for.
  NEITHER PUBLISHED NUMBER HAS BEEN ALTERED. What the manuscript lacks is one
  sentence saying what each includes.
