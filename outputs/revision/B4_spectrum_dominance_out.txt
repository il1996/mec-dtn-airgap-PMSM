=== (iii) spectrum of -Y, and the Laplacian stiffness law ===
  independent spectral formula:
    lambda_m = eps_m (2 mu0 L Ms/pi) sum_{n<=N, n = +-m mod Ms} (kappa_n/n) sin^2(pi n/Ms)
    N=   540 : max relative difference formula vs numerical spectrum = 5.013e-16
    N=  1080 : max relative difference formula vs numerical spectrum = 1.003e-15
    N=  4320 : max relative difference formula vs numerical spectrum = 6.729e-16

    diagonal dominance, is why the condition number settles.
